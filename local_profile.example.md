# Local Profile Addendum

This file extends your taste profile with personal priorities that change more often —
local focus areas, specific interests tied to your current life, and editorial weights
you want to apply to the AI's output.

**Setup:** Copy this file to `local_profile.md` and customise it.
It is combined with `taste_profile.md` at prompt-render time.
It is optional — delete or leave empty to rely solely on `taste_profile.md`.

---

## Geographic focus

List places where local news is personally relevant to you, in priority order.

**[Your primary location]** — your home or immediate area. Any news here is first-person
relevant regardless of how hyperlocal it seems. Surface it; do not filter it out.

**[Your regional hub]** — the nearest city or economic centre. Development, planning,
transport, cultural events, employment — treat this with high relevance.

**[Other places with personal relevance]** — family, work, history. Worth a mention
when significant news occurs.

## Weather

Describe when weather is worth including and when it isn't.

*Example: Only include weather if there is a structurally significant event — prolonged
cold, a storm with practical consequences, a heatwave affecting daily life. "It will be
cloudy" is not worth including.*

---

## [A product or service you use]

Describe what coverage of this product is relevant to you as a *user*, not as a market
observer.

*Example: I own and drive a [car brand]. Coverage is relevant when it concerns things
that directly affect my car: software updates, feature changes, practical owner information.
Company news, financial performance, and executive commentary are not of interest unless
they have a direct bearing on the ownership experience.*

---

## Reading weights

Topics where you want the AI to over- or under-weight relative to raw article count.

*Examples:*
- *Science (physics, biology, mathematics, space): give more depth than article count suggests*
- *Finance and markets: brief mention only, no deep dives*
- *[Topic]: deprioritise — extract any useful insight briefly, but do not dwell*

---

## Editorial orientation

Describe how you want the AI to approach framing and tone across all topics.

*Example: Across all topics, lead with what is constrained, what has gone wrong, what
deserves skepticism, or what has been ruled out — before pivoting to what is positive.
Via negativa (identifying what to rule out) is a thinking tool, not a mood. It is not
the same as doom (dwelling on what is terrible). Use the former, avoid the latter.*
