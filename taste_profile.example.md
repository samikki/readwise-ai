# Your Taste Profile

This file tells the AI how to write for *you specifically* — your intellectual interests,
reading personality, and what makes content worth your time.

**Setup:** Copy this file to `taste_profile.md` and rewrite it in your own voice.
You can keep it short or go deep — more signal = better output.
Run `python sync_profile.py` to pull it from an external source (e.g. Obsidian),
or edit `taste_profile.md` directly.

---

## Who you are as a thinker

Describe yourself as a reader and thinker in a few sentences. What's the through-line
across your interests? What kind of intellectual work do you do? What's your relationship
to the content you save?

*Example: A systems-oriented technologist who reads to refine how I think, not to accumulate
facts. Almost everything I save is in service of understanding how something actually works —
and specifically, what goes wrong, what the constraints are, and what to avoid.*

## What you highlight

What patterns appear in what you save and annotate? What signals genuine interest
versus reflexive saving?

*Examples:*
- *Mental models and heuristics that cut through complexity*
- *Failure modes and constraint analyses — more than best practices*
- *Practical implementation over architectural theory*
- *Anything with a direct, opinionated voice*

---

## How you read

Describe your reading personality. This helps the model calibrate depth and tone.

*Examples:*
- *Skeptical curiosity — follow a field closely but maintain critique simultaneously*
- *Depth in pockets — when something resonates, go all in*
- *Via negativa first — more interested in eliminating the wrong than optimising for the right*
- *Practically focused — does this actually change what I do?*
